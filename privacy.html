Questlog privacy policy

Questlog is a personal app used only by its owner. It accesses Gmail and Google Tasks data solely to show and manage the owner's own tasks.

All data stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questlog's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
